About Clear Flow

Established 2025, Botswana

Established in December 2025, Clear Flow (Pty) Ltd is a premier, wholly citizen-owned engineering services and specialized plumbing company operating within Botswana. We were founded on the principles of environmental stewardship, precision engineering, water conservation, and infrastructural reliability.

Clear Flow addresses critical water and infrastructure challenges faced by property owners, municipalities, commercial operations, and institutions. Our services cover non-invasive underground water leakage detection, plumbing infrastructure support, water purification, and water treatment solutions.

Our specialized mission is to provide practical, reliable solutions that protect water assets, improve water quality, minimize unnecessary losses, and maintain the integrity and performance of water systems.

Specialized Services

Clear Flow (Pty) Ltd delivers an integrated service array covering water leakage detection, plumbing infrastructure, water purification, water treatment, and commercial water network support.

1
Acoustic Underground Water Leakage Detection

Using localized acoustic sensor arrays and highly responsive ground microphones, our technicians trace structural acoustic changes caused by pressurized water escapes. This non-invasive method helps locate leaks beneath concrete slabs, paved areas, and deep earthen corridors.

2
Tracer Gas Diagnostic Methodology

For complex layouts or low-pressure networks where sound waves are muted, we deploy a specialized, non-toxic tracer gas mixture composed of Nitrogen and Hydrogen. The gas is introduced into the isolated system and detected at the point of escape.

3
Commercial & Industrial Fluid Network Maintenance

We execute comprehensive commercial system support, including pressure regulation maintenance, water network line layouts, structural pipeline mapping, and the integration of durable valves and components.

4
Structural Plumbing Services

Our team manages residential and commercial construction plumbing integration, institutional facility maintenance, hot/cold water distribution setups, and emergency infrastructure repairs.

5
Water Purification Services

We provide water purification solutions designed to improve water quality for residential, commercial, institutional, and other suitable applications. Solutions can be tailored to the source water and the intended end use.

6
Water Treatment Services

We provide water treatment solutions focused on addressing water-quality challenges and protecting water systems and equipment. Treatment approaches are selected according to water characteristics, client requirements, and the intended application.
